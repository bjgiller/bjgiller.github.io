Thank you for your interest in the Southwest District Alumni Association (SWDAA)!

Welcome Home

Become part of our growing alumni community

As a joint alumni affiliate of Kappa Kappa Psi and Tau Beta Sigma, SWDAA provides opportunities for alumni to connect, network, and continue serving the Southwest District through regional events, committees, social media, and other alumni programming.

We invite you to become a part of our growing alumni community! To join or renew your membership, please complete the membership form below.

How to Join

Fill out the membership form

Open the membership form

Make your annual contribution

$10 MINIMUM / YEAR

To be considered a member of SWDAA, each individual must pay a minimum annual contribution of $10 for the operating year. However, donations of any amount above the minimum contribution are welcomed and are greatly appreciated. Your contribution makes special programming possible while maintaining a stable financial footing for the organization. If you are a recent graduating senior, no donation fee is required for your first year.

Who Can Join

Membership Eligibility

To be eligible for SWDAA membership, applicants must meet one of the following criteria:

  • Be an Alumni, Life, or Honorary member of Kappa Kappa Psi and/or Tau Beta Sigma with ties to the Southwest District; or
  • Be an Alumni, Life, or Honorary member of the Southwest District of Kappa Kappa Psi and/or Tau Beta Sigma.

Important Membership Information

Per guidelines established by the Kappa Kappa Psi Alumni Association (KKPsiAA) and Tau Beta Sigma Alumni Association (TBSAA), all Kappa Kappa Psi and Tau Beta Sigma members must maintain membership in their respective national alumni association to be eligible for membership in local alumni associations such as SWDAA.
