About the Board

Leadership for our alumni community

The Southwest District Alumni Association Board of Directors provides leadership, support, and direction for the organization and its members. The Board consists of the Chairperson, Membership Director, Programs Director, Communications Director, Finance Director, and two Members-at-Large representing Kappa Kappa Psi and Tau Beta Sigma. The Southwest District Governor(s) and Counselor(s) serve as ex-officio members.

Eligibility

All Board members must be current SWDAA members and members of either the Kappa Kappa Psi Alumni Association or Tau Beta Sigma Alumni Association. Candidates must meet eligibility requirements outlined in the SWDAA Bylaws and are expected to actively support the mission and activities of the Association.

The joint alumni affiliate of Kappa Kappa Psi & Tau Beta Sigma, serving New Mexico, Arkansas, Louisiana, Oklahoma, and Texas.
