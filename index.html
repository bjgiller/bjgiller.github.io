Welcome to SWDAA!

Alumni of the Southwest District of Kappa Kappa Psi & Tau Beta Sigma

The Southwest District Alumni Association provides collaboration and social opportunities for Alumni, Honorary, and Life Members of the Southwest District of Kappa Kappa Psi and Tau Beta Sigma. Alumni serving programs throughout New Mexico, Arkansas, Louisiana, Oklahoma, and Texas!

What We Stand For

Our Three Pillars

Everything SWDAA does comes back to three simple ideas.

01

Excellence

We strive to represent the Southwest District with distinction by supporting outstanding programming, fostering leadership, and advancing the ideals of Kappa Kappa Psi and Tau Beta Sigma at the local, district, and national levels.

02

Connection

We build and strengthen lifelong relationships by bringing alumni together through networking, events, mentorship, and shared experiences.

03

Legacy

We honor our traditions while investing in the future of the Southwest District, ensuring that today's alumni continue to inspire and support generations to come.
